Frequently Asked Questions

How can I place an order?

Browse the collection, add a product to your bag and proceed to checkout.

What payment methods are available?

Cash on Delivery and a UPI option are available in the demo checkout. Actual UPI payment processing is not yet integrated.

How can I check my order?

Open the My Orders page to view orders associated with your current browser session.

What if a product is out of stock?

Products that are out of stock cannot be added to your bag.
